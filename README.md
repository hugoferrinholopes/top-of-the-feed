# Top of the Feed

**Live page:** https://hugoferrinholopes.github.io/top-of-the-feed/

A classroom game on social media feeds, made for GOVT 3421, *Youth and Politics* (Department of Government, Georgetown University, Fall 2026), by Hugo Ferrinho Lopes. The player is the feed editor of an invented students' app.

- **Part 1:** three rounds (Bus fares, Election day, Road blockade). In each round, the player selects the post that goes to the top of the feed and selects Reveal. The page shows the choice's attention, accuracy and agreement, and the post that engagement ranking and newest first would have put on top.
- **Part 2:** the player chooses one rule for the whole feed (most likes and comments first, newest first, trusted sources first, or mix in the other side).

Outcomes are shown as High, Medium or Low. The ranks are illustrative, not measurements. The app, the city and the posts are invented. The page is one self-contained file, `index.html`, and loads nothing from other hosts.
